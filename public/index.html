About Alyssa

From the operating room to global operations.

Alyssa started her career in direct patient care in the ER and OR. She moved into neurology research at Mayo Clinic, then into medical devices at BrainsWay, maker of FDA-cleared Deep TMS systems. There she rose from Field Clinical Engineer to Senior Manager of Global Services, leading a 20-person team across field service and customer success, with customers in 35+ countries. She also earned BrainsWay's Sales Impact Award two years in a row.

That path gives her rare fluency in both clinical reality and commercial pressure. Today she's a PMP-certified contractor with an MPH in Epidemiology. She leads global training for Abbott's diabetes technical operations, runs launch operations for Verastem Oncology, and runs marketing operations for Walmart Connect's go-to-market process.
